EQO identity directions

Three directions for EQO's own look. Each shows the same two real screens from the Phase One flows (setup hub, and an approval with Pause / Stop / Take over) so you can compare them fairly. Pick one, or tell me what to mix.

What EQO is steering away fromOpenDroid's theme file uses pure OLED black or white, zinc greys, neon purple and cyan accents, and monospace text for technical detail. None of the three below use that. EQO's tone is warm, plain-spoken and trustworthy, because the app asks people to hand over screen control and approve sensitive steps.

Mockup only: no app code. All three font families are open-source (SIL OFL), so they can ship inside the APK. Contrast figures are computed live from the hex values; AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text. Light and dark variants of every direction are derived in the next step once you pick.
